### Findings

1. Technology is the strongest category, generating $839,893.28 in sales and $146,543.38 in profit.
2. West is the largest region by both sales and profit, with $739,813.61 in sales and $110,798.82 in profit.
3. Tables require profitability attention: $208,020.18 in sales but a loss of $17,753.21. Bookcases and Supplies also have negative total profit.
4. Annual sales rose from $494,040.21 in 2023 to $745,567.53 in 2026, a 50.9% increase. However, the workbook contains dates beyond the 2026-10-07 execution date, so this is not treated as a current operational trend without a cutoff.
5. Rows with discounts above 20% generated $364,760.15 in sales and a combined loss of $136,020.69. This is an observed association, not proof that discounting caused the losses.

### Dashboard questions

- Which products and customers contribute the most profit, not just sales?
- Which states/cities have high sales but weak margins?
- How does profitability change across discount bands?
- How does performance change after applying a reporting cutoff?
