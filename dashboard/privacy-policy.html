Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

This is a portfolio analytics dashboard displaying publicly derived aggregate data from the Tableau Superstore sample dataset. It was created by Saimanikanta as a demonstration of data analysis and visualization skills. No commercial activity takes place on this site.

2. Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data. This dashboard runs entirely in your browser. There are no cookies, no tracking pixels, and no analytics scripts of any kind. No information about your visit is transmitted to any server.

All data displayed in the charts and tables is pre-computed aggregate data embedded directly in the HTML source. There is no backend, no database, and no user accounts.

3. Third-Party Resources

Chart.js (version 4.4.4) is loaded from the jsDelivr CDN (jsdelivr.net). jsDelivr may log standard CDN access records (IP address, user agent, timestamp) as part of their infrastructure. No other third-party scripts, fonts, or services are used on this dashboard.

Please refer to jsDelivr's Privacy Policy for information about their data practices.
