# Dashboard Specification

## KPI cards
- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Average Order Value

## Visuals
1. Monthly revenue and profit trend
2. Revenue by category
3. Revenue and profit by region
4. Top products by revenue
5. Profitability/weak-segment view

## Filters
Date • Region • Category • Product

## Design principles
- One business question per visual.
- Show units and time period.
- Use consistent number formatting.
- Keep filters clear and minimal.
- Reserve a dedicated area for 3–5 evidence-based findings.

No dashboard result should be presented until it has been calculated from the documented source dataset.
